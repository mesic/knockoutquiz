Privacy Policy

Knockout Quiz · Last updated 16 November 2026

In short: Knockout Quiz does not collect, store or share any personal data. It works fully offline, has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

The player names you type in, your saved game, your settings and the list of questions you have already played are stored only on your device. They are never sent anywhere, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

The full version is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store whether the full version has been purchased on your account.

No network, no third parties

The app makes no network requests of its own and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Children

Because no personal data is collected, the app is suitable for players of all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
