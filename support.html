Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

One person is the quizmaster and holds the iPhone or iPad. They read each question aloud and everyone else shouts their answers. Two correct answers qualify you for the next round; the last player who hasn't qualified is knocked out. The full rules are under How to Play on the home screen.

How many free games do I get?

Three full games with every feature. A game counts once the first player is knocked out, so a game you start and abandon doesn't use one up.

I bought the full version. How do I get it on another device?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is supported.

Can I change how many right answers it takes to qualify?

Yes. When you set up a new game, change Correct answers to qualify. You can also choose categories and difficulty there.

I keep seeing questions I've already played.

Questions you've played are kept out of new games until every question matching your categories and difficulty has been used. Choosing more categories gives more variety. You can reset the history in Settings.

A question has a wrong or unclear answer.

Skip it during the game and email us the question so we can fix it in the next update. Many questions are built from Wikidata.

Tapped the wrong player?

Tap the undo arrow at the top of the game screen. Any action can be undone, including points, knockouts and tie-breakers.

Can I turn off the music?

Yes, in Settings. The menu music also follows your device's silent switch and stops during a game.
