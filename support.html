Knockout Quiz Support

The party trivia game where the last player standing wins.

Need help or found a wrong answer? Email course@fsbitesized.com and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

One person is the quizmaster and holds the phone or iPad. They read each question aloud, and everyone else shouts their answers. Two correct answers qualify you for the next round; the last player who hasn't qualified is knocked out. The full rules are under How to Play on the home screen.

I bought the full version. How do I get it on another device?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is supported.

How many free games do I get?

Three full games, with every feature. A game counts once the first player is knocked out.

I keep seeing questions I've already played.

Questions you have played are kept out of new games until every question matching your chosen categories and difficulty has been used. Choosing more categories gives more variety. You can reset the history in Settings.

A question has a wrong or unclear answer.

Skip it during the game and email us the question, so we can fix it in the next update. Many questions are built from Wikidata.

Is there sound?

Menu music plays on the home screen and can be turned off in Settings. It follows your device's silent switch.
